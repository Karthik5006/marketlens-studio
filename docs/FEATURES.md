# MarketLens Studio 1.0 — features 3–10

## 3. Funds and stock ownership

Exact-ISIN scheme matches, correctly labelled combined shareholder disclosures, promoter/MF/FPI ownership movement and source-extractable pledges. PPFAS plus supported HDFC monthly equity portfolios; other AMCs via XLSX/CSV import. Weighted fund overlap, saved-period position changes, manually entered fund valuations, direct-plus-fund company/industry exposure and explicitly dated user-entered factsheet metrics.

Automatic coverage is not all-AMC. Historical changes start with snapshots actually saved by the app. Expense ratios, scheme returns and tracking error are recorded from user-selected original factsheets, not automatically collected across every AMC.

## 4. Fundamentals and research

Existing filing-backed results plus available multi-period whole-company XBRL statements, profit/cash-flow comparison, capex-based FCF, assets/equity/borrowings, interest coverage and annual ROE/ROCE when sufficient comparable balances exist. Original-statement entry supports missing fields and is explicitly labelled. Loaded-company peer comparisons, filing/annual-report/presentation links, and governance-keyword review filters over the source announcements window.

Taxonomy and filing coverage vary. A missing metric is not zero; a keyword match is not a finding of misconduct. No comprehensive regulatory case database is claimed.

## 5. Discovery and charts

NSE annual extremes, search, watchlist, saved multi-factor screens (P/E, leverage, margin, revenue growth, size, distance from annual high/low, volume, RSI). Universe and loaded-data coverage are shown. Daily OHLC/volume charts with drag inspection, candle/line modes, timeframe selection, SMA/RSI and observed valuation snapshots. Optional explicitly recorded split/bonus chart factors.

Screens are limited to the loaded/selected universe; source requests enrich the first 30 candidates. BSE originals are linked; native BSE data is not included because the endpoint rejected this environment. Charts are raw by default. Split/bonus factors are manual; dividends, rights and demergers are not silently adjusted. Observed P/E history is not a reconstructed long-term series.

## 6. Exposure and risk

Direct and underlying company exposure, source-labelled industry concentration and unclassified/residual values, top holding/top-five concentration, per-stock drawdown and daily volatility, pairwise matched-interval correlation, allocation targets/drift and whole-share magnitudes, plus a clearly labelled uniform-price-shock scenario. Sale previews can estimate lot gains and user-specified tax assumptions.

Source dates differ. Price-risk statistics use raw history and can be affected by corporate actions. Targets cover priced direct equity. No unsupported global/currency classifications or portfolio-wide risk score is invented.

## 7. Alerts

In-app and optional background price thresholds, including returns relative to entered cost, watched/held-stock disclosures and tracked IPO dates. Quiet hours, connected/unmetered setting, approximate interval control, deduplication, background-run status and a persistent inbox. First filing check establishes a baseline.

Android can defer WorkManager jobs; notification permission and network/source access are required. This is not a real-time/exact-deadline notification guarantee. Ownership/fundamental-change push rules and daily digest scheduling are not yet included.

## 8. IPO lifecycle

NSE lists and source series, date/board filters, original issue/offer-document/registrar links, reported bid categories, observed bid snapshots, illustrative lot arithmetic and local document-backed research notes. Application account nicknames, mandate/block/release state, partial allotment, partial sales, net realized profit, blocked-capital overview, exports and tracked-date background reminders.

Lifecycle entries are manual. Automatic allotment/registrar verification, BSE-only native coverage, automatic RHP financial extraction and automatic eligibility/category-rule evaluation are not included. Follow the original issue terms.

## 9. GMP

Separate InvestorGain/IPOWatch reports, original links, row/page/retrieval timestamps and observed history. Missing is distinct from zero. An explicitly confirmed issuer can be paired with a user-entered original-exchange listing outcome, only if this app has an observation before that listing session. No fuzzy issuer joins, publisher averaging, guarantee or GMP-based buy score.

## 10. Dividends and tax records

Dividend ex/record/expected-payment/receipt dates, gross expected versus net received/withholding reconciliation and CSV export. Separate dated acquisition/sale ledger by account, CSV import with ID-based deduplication, acquisition-lot FIFO with fees and oversell validation, financial-year lot reports and hypothetical sales under user-specified rates/cutoffs.

Average-cost positions do not become tax lots. Corporate actions, transfers, grandfathering, exemptions, loss offsets and statutory tax classifications require accurate manual records/review; the app does not claim a filing-ready tax computation. Income records and allotments are not verified against a bank/broker.
